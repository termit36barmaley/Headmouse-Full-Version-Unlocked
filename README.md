## Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

HeadMouse is an innovative tool designed to enable users to control their computer's mouse through head and facial movements. This application is particularly beneficial for individuals with physical disabilities, allowing them to interact with their computer without the need for traditional input devices. By utilizing a webcam, HeadMouse translates head movements into mouse actions such as clicking, dragging, and dropping. Compared to typical alternatives, HeadMouse offers a unique approach that harnesses the capabilities of existing hardware for accessibility.
